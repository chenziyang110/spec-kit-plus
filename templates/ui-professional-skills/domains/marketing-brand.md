# Marketing Brand

This domain covers landing pages, brand pages, campaign pages, hero-led experiences, and visually driven marketing surfaces.

## Apply when

- The request is a landing page, product launch page, brand story, portfolio, campaign page, or homepage hero.
- The goal is persuasion, sign-up, demand generation, or a strong first impression.
- The user wants copy, hierarchy, and imagery to work together as a branded argument.
- The work needs a clear visual point of view, not a neutral app shell.

## Decisions to resolve

- What the page must make the visitor believe in the first screen.
- Which proof points are real and must appear on the page.
- What brand voice, typography, and color temperature fit the subject.
- Whether the page is image-led, type-led, motion-led, or a hybrid.
- How much narrative length the user can tolerate before the CTA.

## Image-primary augmentation

- Prefer image-primary when the agent can generate and inspect visuals.
- Use image prompts for atmosphere, composition, brand feeling, and section rhythm.
- Keep the prompt specific to the campaign subject and avoid generic stock-style marketing language.
- Generate separate section or detail images when a single board becomes too compressed to read.

## HTML-primary augmentation

- Use HTML-primary when image generation is not available or the page must be demoed interactively.
- Keep the prototype focused on real content, tight hero hierarchy, and a single conversion path.
- Use iterative discussion for headline, CTA, social proof, and media framing instead of presenting many rough alternatives.
- Do not invent claims or metrics to make the page look richer.

## Quality gate

- Pass only when the page feels intentional, editorial, and coherent from hero to footer.
- Verify that the CTA, proof, and narrative order are easy to scan.
- Reject layouts that look like a generic SaaS template or a card dump.
- Escalate to the forms, motion, design-system, or accessibility specialists when those concerns dominate the brief.
